## SCENE 3: DESIGN BAY (Dot)
**Dot intro:**
- DOT: You're Walter's kid. You've got his frown.
- DOT: Mug number six. Don't touch it.
- DOT: Rule one: the rocket has to fly straight. Heavy stuff up front, fins at the back, like an arrow.
- DOT: Rule two: enough fuel to get where you're going. Rockets mostly carry fuel to carry fuel.
- DOT: Rule three: one stage won't make orbit. Nobody's does. Stack them.
- DOT: Build it on the bench. Then take it upstairs to the IBM. The computer doesn't lie. People do.

**Objective:** Build BEACON-1

**Dot: "Will it fly?" feedback:**
- Stable & good: Look at that. Your dad would've signed off on it. …Eventually.
- Wobbly (marginal): It'll fly like a drunk pigeon. Bigger fins, or move weight up front.
- Tumbles (unstable): That's not a rocket, that's a pinwheel. Fins. Back. Bigger.
- Over-stable: Too much fin. It'll turn into the wind like a weathervane.
- Not enough fuel: You'll get halfway to orbit and then visit the ocean.
- Too heavy to lift off: It's not leaving the pad. Lighter, or more engine.
- Ready: Run it through the IBM. Then we talk launch.

**Dot: about Dad** (talk again after building):
- DOT: Walter hired me when nobody else would. Brought two coffees every morning, one for me. Every day.
- DOT: He kept a model rocket on his desk. Said a kid designed the fins. Never told me which kid.

**Failure (pick by type):**

- Broke up: EECOM: We've lost telemetry.
